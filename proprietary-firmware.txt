## All proprietary firmware images extracted from realme GT8 Pro (RMX5200_16.0.10.501(CN01)).
##
## These are packaged into the OTA and flashed to both slots (;AB). The set itself
## mirrors OnePlus 15's list - same OPPO SM8850 platform - but every image is
## sourced from the RMX5200 (realme) firmware, never from the OnePlus one.
##
## Deliberately absent: boot, init_boot, vendor_boot, recovery, dtbo, pvmfw and the
## vbmeta* images. Those are built by LineageOS or intentionally left untouched.
##
## All 40 images were verified to exist in the RMX5200_16.0.10.501 payload.
