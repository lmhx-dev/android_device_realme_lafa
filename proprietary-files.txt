## The list above came from device/oneplus/infiniti. Its per-sensor camera
## configs are named after that project (config/infinitimain, ...), so those
## entries did not exist on lafa and were dropped - but the lafa equivalents
## were never added back, leaving /odm/etc/camera only partially listed.
##
## Consequence: odm/etc/camera/config/lafa{main,front,wide,tele} end up missing
## from the build. The camera HAL reads SensorNameList from
## CameraHWConfiguration.config ("lafamain; lafafront; lafawide; lafatele;"),
## cannot find the matching per-sensor configs, falls back to a default sensor
## set and asks the kernel to probe hardware that is not on this device. Every
## sensor then fails cam_sensor_match_id and `dumpsys media.camera` reports
## "Number of camera devices: 0" - the camera app opens to a black screen.
##
## Enumerated by diffing the extracted RMX5200_16.0.10.501 odm tree against the
## one this device tree produces: 256 files, about 1.1 GB. The AI model blobs
## (hybridraw_models, dualcam_capture_bokeh, apk_resources, ...) account for
## most of that; the camera-critical entries are config/lafa*, cfr_bin/CFR_lafa*
## and rmsc_bin/*.
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## OPPO NFC HAL, minimal set, deliberately WITHOUT the VINTF manifest.
##
## The stock NFC stack is OPPO's: nfc_nci.nqx.default.hw.so is the NCI HAL that
## actually opens /dev/nq-nci, served by vendor.oplus.hardware.nfc_aidl-service
## (IOplusNfc). LOS ships only its own android.hardware.nfc-service.nxp /
## nfc_nci_nxp_snxxx.so, which loads but never opens the device - NfcService then
## reports "NFC is not initialized yet" forever and mState stays off.
##
## The manifest (manifest_oplus_nfc_aidl.xml) is left out on purpose: adding it
## in the earlier batch made the device hang on the boot screen, because the
## declared HAL instance never came up. The AIDL service registers itself with
## servicemanager regardless.
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## NXP HIDL NFC interface libs - hard deps of nfc_nci.nqx.default.hw

## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## The vendor NFC extension bridge.
##
## packages/modules/Nfc/libnfc-nci/src/nfc_vendor_extn/NfcVendorExtn.cc does
##     mLibPathName = findLibPath();
##     if (mLibPathName.empty()) { LOG(ERROR) << "Failed to find %s !!"; return false; }
## and findLibPath() tries, in order:
##     1. persist.nfc_vendor_extn.lib_file_name
##     2. libnfc_vendor_extn-<ro.boot.product.hardware.sku>.so
##     3. libnfc_vendor_extn.so
## All three are absent here, so NfcExtn_LibSetup() fails, NfcVendorExtn::
## Initialize() returns false and NfcService logs "enableInternal: Error
## enabling NFC" - NFC never enables, mState stays off, and the HAL is never
## asked to open /dev/nq-nci. Symptom in logcat:
##     libnfc_nci: NfcExtn_LibSetup: Failed to find  !!
## (note the empty name - that is mLibPathName being empty).
##
## The library only needs platform libs plus vendor.nxp.nxpnfc_aidl.INxpNfc,
## which LineageOS already ships and runs, so no OPPO service or extra sepolicy
## is required.
## ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
